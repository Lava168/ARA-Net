## Methods Insert

To support reproducible review of the locked probability ensemble, we implemented a browser-based ARA-Net Research Workbench. The workbench accepts the same base-model CN/MCI/AD probability CSV format used by the command-line inference wrapper, calls the locked `/predict` research API, aggregates repeated scans at the subject level when requested, and displays class probabilities, confidence, decision margin, cohort metadata, and exportable prediction tables. A separate MRI-file staging panel accepts common research imaging formats, including NIfTI and DICOM, and displays an MRI-to-atlas-to-probability workflow status. The revised clinical-exhibit section adds reviewer-facing evidence that is commonly expected in clinical AI papers: deployment-side size and runtime metrics for the final inference head, atlas-level AD-prior regional consistency, and external healthy-control specificity. This panel is intended to illustrate the planned review workflow and does not currently replace upstream MRI preprocessing, atlas-feature extraction, or base-model probability generation. The interface does not provide clinical diagnosis; it is intended to demonstrate a reproducible research workflow for retrospective evaluation and future prospective validation studies.

## Discussion Insert

The Research Workbench illustrates how the ARA-Net evidence chain could be inspected in a deployment-facing research setting while preserving clinical-use boundaries. The interface exposes subject-level predictions, probability margins, atlas-region context, MRI workflow status, and external-validation status rather than returning a single opaque label. This design is intended to make uncertainty, cohort role, and claim boundaries visible during review. However, the current implementation is a research prototype: MRI upload is used for workflow staging and visualization, whereas locked prediction is performed from upstream probability inputs. The interface is not a clinically validated device and would require prospective multi-center testing, local calibration, cybersecurity review, workflow integration, and regulatory assessment before any clinical use.
