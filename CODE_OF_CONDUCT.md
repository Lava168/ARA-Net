# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ARA-Net project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing scientific opinions and implementation choices.
- Keep discussion focused on reproducible research, documentation, and software quality.
- Be careful with clinical, patient, and dataset-use boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing restricted data, subject identifiers, or private model artifacts.
- Presenting this research prototype as a cleared clinical diagnostic device.
- Disruptive or bad-faith conduct in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent participation limits.

## Reporting

Report concerns through the repository issue tracker or by contacting the project maintainer. Maintainers should handle reports with care, confidentiality, and proportionality.
